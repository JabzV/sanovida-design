# SANOVIDA.MX — Project Brief

**SANOVIDA.MX** is a new brand in the weight-loss sector. It sells **Orforglipron** pills together with a personalized app for tracking the dieting journey.

---

## 1. Products

Orforglipron pills come in five dosages:

| Dosage |
|--------|
| 0.8 mg |
| 2.5 mg |
| 5.5 mg |
| 9 mg   |
| 14.5 mg |

Each dosage comes in branded, premium-designed packaging.

## 2. Purchase Packages

There are three packages:

- **1 month**
- **3 months**
- **6 months**

## 3. Free SANOVIDA.MX App

Every customer who buys a package gets free access to the SANOVIDA.MX app. The app is a white-label solution acquired from **Harbiz**.

The app includes:

- Weight and progress tracking
- Nutrition plans
- Workout plans
- Charts and body measurements
- Reminders and other tools that support the journey

## 4. Website Structure (One Page)

The website is a **single landing page**: concise, high-end, and focused on sales.

| # | Section | Content |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Main Banner** | Introduces the brand and product, with a purchase button. |
| 2 | **Our Product** | A short explanation of Orforglipron and the five available dosages. |
| 3 | **Choose Your Package** | Choose a 1, 3, or 6-month plan. |
| 4 | **Free SANOVIDA.MX App** | The app's benefits, shown with images. |
| 5 | **Checkout** | A quick, simple purchase process. |

**Out of scope:** separate product pages or long explanations. The page should show the customer all key information in one place and lead them to a purchase as quickly as possible.

> **Decision (2026-10-26):** the site is built as **five pages** that follow the reference screens in `references/Landing Page/Desktop View/1–5.png`: Inicio, Producto, Paquetes, App and Checkout, plus a Preguntas frecuentes page for the nav link. The home page still carries every key section (product, packages, app, how it works) so a visitor can buy from it without visiting the others.

## 5. Languages

- **Spanish (Mexico)**: default
- **English**: additional option

The website must be fully optimized for desktop and mobile.

## 6. Customer Experience

After purchase, the customer receives:

1. An order confirmation.
2. Access to the SANOVIDA.MX app, where they can track progress and use the support tools.

## 7. Marketing Concept

The brand is built around the concept **"8 kilos in 10 days."**

> ⚠️ **Compliance note:** This message must **not** be presented as a guaranteed result without proper substantiation. Copy should frame it as a concept or goal, with appropriate disclaimers.

---

## Goal

Build a **premium, minimalist, conversion-focused** website where the customer can quickly understand the product, choose a package, and complete the purchase.

### Competitive Advantage

The customer gets more than weight-loss pills. They get an integrated solution under the SANOVIDA.MX brand:

- The product (Orforglipron)
- Nutrition plans
- Workout plans
- A personalized tracking app
