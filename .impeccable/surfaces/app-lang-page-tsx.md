Scope: the six marketing pages (`/`, `/producto`, `/paquetes`, `/app`, `/checkout`, `/preguntas-frecuentes`, and their `/en` twins). Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

Audience: women in Mexico on a phone, deciding in one visit. Action: choose dosage and package, then check out. Proof: the full program (product, nutrition, workouts, app), not claims. Constraints: the user's reference screens (`references/Landing Page/Desktop View/1–5.png`) are binding and the build must stay very close to them; no unproven claims; "8 kilos en 10 días" only as a concept with a disclaimer; placeholder images until the user supplies them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A faithful build of the reference: a sunlit Mediterranean "pharmacy-boutique" with gold eyebrows, serif headlines with a rose italic accent, gold pill buttons and colour-coded dosage boxes. It refuses the generic diet-pill funnel (countdowns, before/after, stacked badges).
OWN-WORLD: white and cream ground, deep green ink, rose accent, gold CTA gradient, five dosage colours (sky, cobalt, jade, gold, magenta). Ibarra Real Nova display, Figtree body, Caveat hand notes.
STORY: every page opens with the same hero grammar (eyebrow, headline, lead, ring benefits, gold CTA, trust row, hand note top right), then its own sections, then a closing CTA band. Buy buttons carry the chosen package and dosage into the checkout.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed photo (A1 on every page, A5 on the app page; the dosage boxes are composited from B1–B5) with copy on the left 45% and the hand-written note top right, exactly as the reference.
FORM: user-pinned reference world (beats the roll; no seed). Signature interaction: the checkout summary lets the buyer switch package and dosage in place and the totals update.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
