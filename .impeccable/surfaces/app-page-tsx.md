Scope: the single landing page (`/` es-MX, `/en`). Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

Audience: women in Mexico on a phone, deciding in one visit. Action: choose dosage and package, then check out on the same page. Proof: the full program (product, nutrition, workouts, app), not claims. Constraints: no unproven claims, "8 kilos en 10 días" only as a concept with a disclaimer, placeholder images until the user supplies them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Mediterranean "pharmacy-boutique" morning. The page refuses the generic diet-pill funnel (countdowns, before/after photos, stacked badges) and sells calm, light, and a complete program.
OWN-WORLD: white-and-linen ground, deep olive-green ink, a rose accent, metallic gold call-to-action buttons, and five dosage colors (sky, cobalt, jade, ochre, rose). Ibarra Real Nova serif display with a rose italic accent, Figtree body, a Caveat hand note.
STORY: understand the offer in the hero, learn the product, choose a dosage, choose a package, see the app as part of the product, then pay. Every CTA leads to checkout with the selection already filled in.
FIRST VIEWPORT: a full-bleed sunlit photo (A1) with the woman on the right third and the boxes in front. Left: a three-line serif headline, one sentence of copy, a gold CTA, and a trust row. Top right: the hand-written concept "8 kilos en 10 días*".
FORM: user-pinned reference world (beats the roll; no seed). Signature interaction: the dosage selector recolors the product stage, and the choice carries into checkout.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
